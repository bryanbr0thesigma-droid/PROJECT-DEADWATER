# PROJECT: DEADWATER: lore (draft)

Status: brainstorm. **Canon** marks the creator's own lore. Everything else is a proposal, and
none of it is in the game yet unless it says so.

**In one line:** A-Tech opened the door. Dr. Bryant took F.A.L.C.O.N. through it and deeper, to make
money, and the people who find out how are disposed of. The story is F.A.L.C.O.N.'s.

Tone: a cheerful organisation hiding something rotten. You find out through its own tapes, memos and
marketing, and its voice never cracks however bad the truth gets.

---

## Canon: how it started

- **October 17, 1989:** A-Tech uses the **Low-Proximity Magnetic Distortion System** to open a
  portal to the complex.
- **A-Tech stops at Level 0** (the complex).
- **June 24, 1990:** A-Tech's co-founder, **Dr. Bryant**, splits off, believing it has untapped potential. He founds
  **F.A.L.C.O.N.** and **deepens it**, pushing the machine past Level 0 and into the poolrooms.
- **A-Tech and F.A.L.C.O.N. are separate corporate entities.** Both still exist.
- **A-Tech doesn't know F.A.L.C.O.N. exists.** F.A.L.C.O.N. knows about A-Tech, loosely: it keeps
  half an eye on its old partner.
- **The story focuses on F.A.L.C.O.N.** A-Tech stays in the background.

> **A deliberate nod.** A-Tech, the machine's name, the date and "Level 0" point at a well-known Backrooms
> series on purpose, so fans make the connection. It's knock-off naming, like the lobby's
> Chlora-Cola: "A-Tech", never the original company's name. Keep it exactly as written; don't
> "fix" it.

## Canon: what happens to people who find out

Whoever finds out what F.A.L.C.O.N. is really doing is **disposed of**. They are **not** turned
into Waders.

## Canon: the game turns into an escape

As the players learn the truth, the game starts leaning into two things at once: **surviving** it,
and **trying to escape your predicament**, getting out from under F.A.L.C.O.N. (Proposal: the turn
comes at layer 5 of the iceberg below.)

## Canon: the Wader isn't the only one

There are more Waders, in **groups**, in areas you'll never access.

## Proposed: F.A.L.C.O.N. today

- **Pinewater Field Station.** F.A.L.C.O.N. builds its own copy of the machine in the basement of
  a closed hotel, bought as cover. The hotel's reception is the lobby.
- **The name is a costume.** "Federal Agency for Liminal Classification & Occult Nature" is a
  private company dressed up as a government agency, so nobody asks questions.
- **The untapped potential:** the poolrooms are outside every jurisdiction. There's no law, no
  witnesses and no end, and bodies never turn up. F.A.L.C.O.N. runs the place as a black site.

## Proposed: F.A.L.C.O.N.'s two businesses

1. **Disappearances for hire.** Clients pay to make people vanish. "Visitors" are escorted in
   and never sign out. Tape 08-E already says it: *"Personnel who do not sign out are
   considered still inside."*
2. **Human trials.** The "Training Division" is a lab, and its recruits are the subjects,
   picked because nobody will miss them. The ads ("Steady work. Federal pension.") target people
   with no family, and the pension is never paid. Every round is a trial that measures how
   people last against the Wader. The results go to buyers.

## Proposed: the rest of F.A.L.C.O.N.

- **The Waders** were already in the poolrooms (Liminal Entity L-7, one of the things in the lobby's
  field guide, L-1 to L-9). They live in groups. F.A.L.C.O.N. cuts one off from its group and
  shuts it in each trial area: the one you face is alone, and it's the trial.
- **Deadwater**, the glowing mineral water, is just what runs the generators. It isn't the secret.

## Proposed: the groups, glimpsed

Every level gets at least one glimpse of a group of Waders somewhere players can never reach:

- several tall shapes standing still in a far-off flooded hall, seen through a drain grate;
- ripples moving together, in step, across an unlit pool behind a locked fence;
- a lidar scan through a gap in a wall that shows six figures where there should be none;
- clicking from behind a sealed door: many voices at once, answering each other;
- through thick observation glass in a sealed staff corridor, a group wading slowly past.

They never come closer, and they're never explained on the spot. Later, F.A.L.C.O.N. paperwork
does: colony maps, head counts ("Sector 9: group of 12. Do not open."), and the method for cutting
one loose for a trial. Cheap to build: the creature model idling far away, or just the sound.

Idea: when your Wader clicks, a group somewhere far off sometimes clicks back.

## Proposed: how F.A.L.C.O.N. words it

On paper, disposal is "**reassignment to Deep Survey**". In practice they're sent alone into a
sealed sector, near a group. The paperwork stays polite: "Thank you for volunteering for Deep Survey! Your locker will
be cleared for the next recruit."

## Proposed: the iceberg (the deeper you dig, the more you find)

| Depth | What you find | F.A.L.C.O.N.'s voice |
|---|---|---|
| **0. The brochure** (the lobby) | The warm lobby, recruitment posters, a pension plan, Chlora-Cola, caring orientation tapes. | "We keep you safe." |
| **1. The fine print** (the Shallow Halls, in plain sight) | Sign-in entries with no time out. "Wet footprints? Report them." Grief counselling "for your family". The rules start to protect F.A.L.C.O.N., not you. | Friendly, a little off. |
| **2. The memos** (the Deep End, staff rooms) | People become numbers: "Team 14: losses 2. Data: excellent." Visitor passes with no names. | "Remember to thank your team for their contribution! 🙂" |
| **3. The business** (locked rooms, cabinets) | Client contracts ("one visitor, no return"). Trial reports sold to buyers. A sales tape. The A-Tech split, and Dr. Bryant's pitch. F.A.L.C.O.N.'s thin file on A-Tech. | A smiley sticker on an invoice. |
| **4. Deep Survey** (hidden, hard to reach) | The colony maps and head counts that explain the groups you've glimpsed. Personnel files stamped REASSIGNED. The whistleblower's tapes. | "Thank you for volunteering for Deep Survey!" |
| **5. The bottom** (only for players who dig hardest) | A reassignment list with **your team on it**: "Training Division team. Flagged: unauthorised access. Reassign after next trial." Now you know, and so do they. **From here the goal is to escape.** | "Congratulations on your assignment." |

The rules that make this work:

- **Physically deeper means a deeper secret.** Each level goes further into the poolrooms.
- **The front never cracks.** The darker the find, the cheerier the packaging.
- **Digging is optional.** The main path shows layers 0 to 2; layers 3 to 5 are for players who
  search (hidden tapes, vents, codes, locked cabinets).
- **Each find changes what you've already seen.** Rewatch tape 07-W after layer 5, and "Thank you
  for your service" means something else.

## Proposed: the whistleblower

**Dr. M. Halloran** (placeholder name) is a F.A.L.C.O.N. scientist who recorded the truth on blank
VHS tapes and hid them in the levels. Players bring them back to the lobby VCR (which already
exists). Halloran was reassigned. The last tape is recorded in Deep Survey, and a group can be
heard clicking closer in the background.

## Proposed: the Wader's clicks

The tapes say "Listen for its clicking". F.A.L.C.O.N.'s trial notes break the clicks into a code the
players can learn:

- **patrol:** single clicks;
- **investigate:** double clicks;
- **charge:** three, or one long rattle.

## Proposed: the levels, each one deeper

| Level | Layers |
|---|---|
| Lobby (the hotel over the machine) | 0, and 5 once you know |
| The Shallow Halls (built) | 1 |
| The Deep End (gray-box) | 2, and the first Halloran tape |

| Deep Survey (future): the sealed sectors | 4 |

## How it gets into the game

- **The sign-in book remembers the dead.** When a player dies in a round, their name appears in the
  lobby's register with no time out. Cheap, personal, and a good first piece to build.
- **Glimpses of the groups**, at least one per level.
- **Hidden tapes:** Halloran's, collected in the levels and played in the lobby.
- **New lobby tapes:**
  - Tape 09, a cheerful pitch to clients;
  - Tape 00, footage of F.A.L.C.O.N.'s first trip through the machine in 1990.
- **Props:** clipboards, memos, personnel files and client contracts (the named-prop items already
  exist).
- **Layer 5 changes the game (canon direction; details proposed):** once your team has found the
  reassignment list, the lobby turns on you a little each round. Your lockers are cleared, and a
  "Deep Survey" sign-up sheet has your names already filled in. Rounds now have two goals: survive
  the trial, and work towards a way out.
- **The way out (proposal):** not back through F.A.L.C.O.N.'s station, but *up*: back towards
  Level 0, where A-Tech's portal still stands. A-Tech doesn't know F.A.L.C.O.N. exists, so
  arriving at its door is the last thing anyone expects, and the fan connection pays off.
