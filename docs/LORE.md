# PROJECT: DEADWATER: lore (draft)

Status: brainstorm. **Canon** marks the creator's own lore. Everything else is a proposal, and
none of it is in the game yet unless it says so.

**In one line:** A-Tech opened the door. Dr. Bryant took F.A.L.C.O.N. through it and deeper, to
exploit what's down there, and whoever finds out is disposed of. The story is F.A.L.C.O.N.'s.

Tone: a cheerful organisation hiding something rotten. You find out through its own tapes, memos and
marketing, and its voice never cracks however bad the truth gets.

---

## Canon

### How it started

- **October 17, 1989:** A-Tech uses the **Low-Proximity Magnetic Distortion System** to open a
  portal to the complex.
- **A-Tech stops at Level 0** (the complex).
- **June 24, 1990:** A-Tech's co-founder, **Dr. Bryant**, splits off, believing it has untapped
  potential. He founds **F.A.L.C.O.N.** and **deepens it**, pushing the machine past Level 0 and into
  the poolrooms.

> **A deliberate nod.** A-Tech, the machine's name, the date and "Level 0" point at a well-known
> Backrooms series on purpose, so fans make the connection. It's knock-off naming, like the lobby's
> Chlora-Cola: "A-Tech", never the original company's name. Keep it exactly as written; don't
> "fix" it.

### The two companies

- **A-Tech and F.A.L.C.O.N. are separate corporate entities.** Both still exist.
- **A-Tech doesn't know F.A.L.C.O.N. exists.** F.A.L.C.O.N. knows about A-Tech, loosely.
- **The story focuses on F.A.L.C.O.N.** A-Tech stays in the background.

### F.A.L.C.O.N.

- It **found** the poolrooms (through the deepened portal) and is **exploiting** them.
- Whoever finds out what it's really doing is **disposed of**. They are **not** turned into Waders.
- **Dr. Bryant appears in the game at some point.**

### The Waders

- There are more Waders, in **groups**, in areas you'll never access.

### Escaping

- As the players learn the truth, the game starts leaning into two things at once: **surviving** it,
  and **trying to escape your predicament**.
- **F.A.L.C.O.N.'s portal leads to the exit.**

## Proposed

### The station

**Pinewater Field Station:** F.A.L.C.O.N.'s portal is in the basement of a closed hotel, bought as
cover. The hotel's reception is the lobby. The name "Federal Agency for Liminal Classification &
Occult Nature" is a costume: a private company dressed up as a government agency, so nobody asks
questions.

### The groups, glimpsed

Every level gets at least one glimpse of a group of Waders somewhere players can never reach:

- several tall shapes standing still in a far-off flooded hall, seen through a drain grate;
- ripples moving together, in step, across an unlit pool behind a locked fence;
- a lidar scan through a gap in a wall that shows six figures where there should be none;
- clicking from behind a sealed door: many voices at once, answering each other;
- through thick observation glass in a sealed staff corridor, a group wading slowly past.

They never come closer, and they're never explained on the spot. Later, F.A.L.C.O.N. paperwork does
(colony maps, head counts like "Sector 9: group of 12. Do not open."). Cheap to build: the creature
model idling far away, or just the sound.

The Wader you face is a lone one, cut off from its group. When it clicks, a group somewhere far off
sometimes clicks back.

### How F.A.L.C.O.N. words disposal

On paper it's "**reassignment to Deep Survey**". The paperwork stays polite: "Thank you for
volunteering for Deep Survey! Your locker will be cleared for the next recruit."

### The iceberg: the deeper you dig, the more you find

| Depth | What you find | F.A.L.C.O.N.'s voice |
|---|---|---|
| **0. The brochure** (the lobby) | The warm lobby, recruitment posters, a pension plan, Chlora-Cola, caring orientation tapes. | "We keep you safe." |
| **1. The fine print** (the Shallow Halls, in plain sight) | Sign-in entries with no time out. "Wet footprints? Report them." Grief counselling "for your family". The rules start to protect F.A.L.C.O.N., not you. | Friendly, a little off. |
| **2. The memos** (the Deep End, staff rooms) | People become numbers: "Team 14: losses 2. Acceptable." | "Remember to thank your team for their contribution! 🙂" |
| **3. The project** (locked rooms, cabinets) | What F.A.L.C.O.N. is really exploiting the poolrooms for. The A-Tech split, and Dr. Bryant's pitch. F.A.L.C.O.N.'s thin file on A-Tech. | A smiley sticker on something awful. |
| **4. Deep Survey** (hidden, hard to reach) | The colony maps that explain the groups you've glimpsed. Files stamped REASSIGNED. The whistleblower's tapes. | "Thank you for volunteering for Deep Survey!" |
| **5. The bottom** (only for players who dig hardest) | A reassignment list with **your team on it**. Now you know, and so do they. **From here, the goal is to escape through F.A.L.C.O.N.'s portal.** | "Congratulations on your assignment." |

The rules that make this work:

- **Physically deeper means a deeper secret.** Each level goes further into the poolrooms.
- **The front never cracks.** The darker the find, the cheerier the packaging.
- **Digging is optional.** The main path shows layers 0 to 2; layers 3 to 5 are for players who
  search (hidden tapes, vents, codes, locked cabinets).
- **Each find changes what you've already seen.** Rewatch tape 07-W after layer 5, and "Thank you
  for your service" means something else.

### The escape

Once your team is on the list, the station turns on you a little each round. Your lockers are
cleared, and a "Deep Survey" sign-up sheet has your names already filled in. Rounds now have two
goals: survive, and work towards F.A.L.C.O.N.'s portal and the exit, with F.A.L.C.O.N. trying to stop
you.

### The whistleblower

**Dr. M. Halloran** (placeholder name) is a F.A.L.C.O.N. scientist who recorded the truth on blank
VHS tapes and hid them in the levels. Players bring them back to the lobby VCR (which already
exists). Halloran was "reassigned". The last tape is recorded in Deep Survey, and a group can be
heard clicking closer in the background.

### The Wader's clicks

The tapes say "Listen for its clicking". F.A.L.C.O.N.'s notes break the clicks into a code the
players can learn:

- **patrol:** single clicks;
- **investigate:** double clicks;
- **charge:** three, or one long rattle.

### The levels, each one deeper

| Level | Layers |
|---|---|
| Lobby (the hotel over the portal) | 0, and 5 once you know |
| The Shallow Halls (built) | 1 |
| The Deep End (gray-box) | 2, and the first Halloran tape |
| Future level | 3 |
| Deep Survey (future) | 4 |
| The bottom (finale) | 5, then the escape |

### How it gets into the game

- **The sign-in book remembers the dead.** When a player dies in a round, their name appears in the
  lobby's register with no time out ("Personnel who do not sign out are considered still
  inside"). Cheap, personal, and a good first piece to build.
- **Glimpses of the groups**, at least one per level.
- **Hidden tapes:** Halloran's, collected in the levels and played in the lobby.
- **New lobby tapes:** for example, Tape 00, footage of F.A.L.C.O.N.'s first trip through the portal
  in 1990.
- **Props:** clipboards, memos and files (the named-prop items already exist).
